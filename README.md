## The entry (124 characters, 21 words, one line)

> One star. Monday served my weekend cold, charged me for the coffee, and the waiter — my alarm — arrived forty minutes early.

## Why it works

A one-star restaurant review where Monday is the restaurant: the weekend served cold, coffee charged for, and the waiter turns out to be your alarm clock arriving forty minutes early. Three concrete complaints in one breath, each one true for every office worker, and the twist — the waiter is the alarm — lands last and hardest.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python stdlib only, no network, no clock) writes the line to `entry.txt` in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (fallback `/output`), deterministic and byte-identical across runs, with no trailing newline. It gate-checks the line before writing: 1–180 characters, at most 25 words, a single line, non-blank, no leading/trailing whitespace.

Run it:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 -P -s main.py
```
